About the Challenge

Human infants acquire language from sparse, weakly-aligned multimodal input — yet today's vision–language models (VLMs), trained on curated web data, fail to generalize to the kinds of egocentric streams produced by wearable cameras and head-cams. The EgoBabyVLM Challenge invites the community to develop models capable of grounded language learning from naturalistic infant egocentric video.

Participants train on a fixed-size baby egocentric video corpus and are evaluated on three families of tasks: cross-modal language grounding via our Machine-DevBench suite, unimodal vision tasks (object recognition and visual properties), and unimodal language tasks (syntax and semantics). A successful submission must not only acquire grounded language, but also maintain or improve unimodal encoder quality through cross-modal training.

Instructions

1

Get the data

Download the fixed-size BabyView training corpus (frames + transcribed utterances) and the Machine-DevBench evaluation suite. Use of any additional supervised data is not allowed beyond pretrained encoders listed in the rules.

Download tar →
2

Train your model

Architectures may be contrastive (CLIP+-style) or generative (LLaVA-style). You may initialize from public pretrained encoders, but multimodal alignment training must use only the released BabyView corpus. Report ≥ 3 seeds.

3

Evaluate

Run the provided evaluation pipeline to produce cross-modal (Machine-DevBench), unimodal vision, and unimodal language scores. Report Lexical, Grammatical, and Overall aggregates per family (mean ± std).

4

Submit

Open a pull request adding your entry to leaderboard.json, plus a short technical report (≤ 2 pages) describing your approach.

Submitting Your Results

  1. Run the evaluation pipeline on your trained model.
  2. Fork this repository and add your entry to leaderboard.json with the following fields:
    • model — name of your model
    • team — your team / affiliation
    • architecture — contrastive or generative
    • training_data — corpus(es) used (must include BabyView for the official challenge track)
    • multimodal — {lexical, grammatical, overall} mean ± std on Machine-DevBench
    • vision — {object_recognition, visual_properties, overall} mean ± std
    • language — {syntax, semantics, overall} mean ± std (specify the text encoder used)
    • report_url — link to your technical report
    • code_url — link to a public code release (optional but recommended)
  3. Open a pull request. Organizers will verify your submission and update the leaderboard.
